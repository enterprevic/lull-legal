Lull

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

The short version: Lull has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number. Your mixes, nights, journal and companion stay on your device. We use RevenueCat to check subscriptions, and (if you leave it on) PostHog for anonymous usage statistics. No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your data.

This policy explains how Novek Labs ("we", "us") handles information in the Lull app for Android and iOS ("Lull"). Contact: victor@noveklabs.com.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you create or choose in Lull is stored only on your device: saved mixes, favorites, listening and breathing sessions, focus sessions, sleep journal entries, wake times, your companion's name and look, and your settings. We do not receive it. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Erase All Data, removes it.

Reminders, Gentle Wake alarms and focus alerts are scheduled locally on your device. Lull does not use push notifications from a server.

2. Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details. To know whether you have Premium, Lull uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • a random app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name or email),
  • your purchase and subscription history from the store (product, dates, status, price and currency),
  • basic device and app information (operating system, app version, country and language), and your IP address as part of the network request.

This is used only to provide and restore your purchases, and to give us totals such as how many people subscribe.

3. Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog)

To learn which parts of Lull are useful, we use PostHog. It receives events such as "onboarding step viewed", "paywall viewed", "sound played" or "focus session finished", which screen was open, and basic device information (device model, operating system, app version, language). Events are tied to a random install ID, not to you. We do not send anything you type, your companion's name, your journal or your listening history in detail, and we have set PostHog not to store IP addresses.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share Anonymous Usage. Nothing is sent while it is off.

4. What Lull does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email or phone number.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location access.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • Lull is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat sleep conditions.

5. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above (RevenueCat, PostHog, and Google or Apple for payments), who process it on our behalf, or when required by law. Share images you create in Lull are made on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

6. Where data is processed and how long it is kept

RevenueCat and PostHog process data in the United States. Purchase records are kept while needed to provide your subscription and for legal and accounting purposes. Anonymous usage events are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Because Lull has no accounts, we usually cannot identify you; on-device data is fully in your control. For anything held by our providers, email victor@noveklabs.com and we will help. Our legal bases are providing the service you asked for (subscriptions) and our legitimate interest in improving Lull (anonymous statistics, which you can switch off). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Lull is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). On-device data is protected by your device's own security.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app.
